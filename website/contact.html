Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can we start?

Most engagements begin within 2 weeks of signing. For urgent needs, we can often start within days.

Do you work with companies outside the US?

Yes. We work with companies globally, with particular expertise in US and EU regulatory environments.

What if we're not sure what we need?

That's what the discovery call is for. We'll help you clarify your challenges and recommend the right starting point—even if that's not with us.

Can you work with our existing AI tools and vendors?

Absolutely. We're platform-agnostic and often help companies get more value from tools they already own.

What's your typical engagement size?

Most projects range from $25K-$150K, with monthly retainers from $3K-$15K. We're transparent about pricing—no surprise costs.

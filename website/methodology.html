How We Use VN-1

VN-1 isn't just a checklist—it's a thinking system. Each lens represents a different mode of strategic analysis:

  • Vision — Inspired by design thinking. Does this create emotional resonance?
  • Mission — Grounded in stakeholder impact. Who wins, who loses?
  • System — Focused on long-term dynamics. What's the flywheel effect?
  • Resilience — Stress-tested against scenarios. What breaks first?
  • Execution — Mapped to operational reality. How do humans and AI collaborate?

Decision Rules

Minimum Approval

3 of 5 lenses must score positively for a GO decision.

Veto Threshold

If 2+ lenses score critically negative, escalate for deeper review.

Tie-Breaker

Mission lens breaks ties on purpose-alignment. Resilience breaks ties on risk.

Human Touchpoints

Decision Validation Gates

Critical decisions require human approval before execution. We define which decisions, who approves, and the SLA.

Exception Escalation Paths

When AI confidence drops below threshold or encounters edge cases, clear escalation to human supervisors.

Model Behavior Review

Regular sampling and review of AI outputs by domain experts. Feedback loops into continuous improvement.

Compliance Checkpoints

Regulatory-required human oversight at defined intervals. Full audit trail for every decision.

HITL Role Definitions

Clear roles make human oversight operationally sustainable.

AI Supervisor

Responsibility: Day-to-day oversight of AI operations

Background: Operations manager + AI training

Key Tasks: Monitor dashboards, handle escalations, document exceptions

Model Behavior Coach

Responsibility: Fine-tuning AI outputs based on feedback

Background: Domain expert + ML familiarity

Key Tasks: Review samples, provide corrections, validate improvements

Algorithm Ethics Officer

Responsibility: Ensuring fairness and compliance

Background: Legal/compliance + AI ethics

Key Tasks: Policy enforcement, bias detection, regulatory liaison

HITL Program Manager

Responsibility: Overall human oversight program

Background: Project management + AI governance

Key Tasks: Resource planning, metrics reporting, process improvement

HITL Metrics Framework

What gets measured gets managed. We track these key indicators:

📊

Volume

AI decisions per human review. Establish baseline, then optimize.

✅

Quality

Human override rate. Target: <5% (indicates good AI calibration).

⚡

Speed

Time to human escalation. Target: <2 minutes for critical.

📋

Compliance

Audit-ready documentation rate. Target: 100%.

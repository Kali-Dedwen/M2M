About M2M

We exist to bridge the gap between AI adoption and AI value.

The Problem We Saw

Every company we talked to had the same story: they'd adopted AI tools, hired consultants, maybe even built internal teams—but they weren't seeing results.

The data confirmed it: 88% of organizations now use AI regularly, but only 6% achieve meaningful business impact. That's a 94% failure rate on what's supposed to be transformative technology.

We dug deeper. The problem wasn't the technology—it was the approach:

  • Big consulting firms over-engineered solutions that mid-market companies couldn't maintain
  • Strategy consultants delivered decks, not working systems
  • AI tools were deployed without human oversight frameworks
  • Governance was an afterthought, not architecture

So we built M2M to solve these problems. Human+AI systems that actually work, deployed in 90 days, with oversight built in from day one.

Why We Focus on Mid-Market

Companies with $10M-$500M in revenue face a unique challenge: they need enterprise-grade AI capabilities but can't afford enterprise-grade consulting budgets or multi-year timelines.

The Big 4 systematically over-engineer solutions for these companies. Their teams are trained to think in terms of enterprise-wide transformation, not surgical improvements.

We're different. We understand resource constraints. We know you can't dedicate a 20-person team to an AI initiative. We build solutions that your existing team can maintain and evolve.

Our sweet spot: Companies in healthcare, financial services, legal, and manufacturing—industries where AI impact is high and human oversight is non-negotiable.
